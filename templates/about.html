About the Project

Project Overview

DustMonitor is an academic project designed to analyze the concentration of dust (specifically PM2.5 and PM10 particulate matter) in households located near main roads. By integrating environmental data and machine learning, this project aims to understand how various factors contribute to air pollution.

Problem Statement

“Households near major roads may experience dust and particulate pollution because of traffic, road conditions, construction and weather conditions. This project analyzes these factors to understand dust concentration.”

Objectives

  • Analyze dust concentration
  • Study environmental factors
  • Analyze PM2.5 and PM10
  • Train machine learning models
  • Predict dust concentration
  • Provide environmental recommendations
