Environmental Recommendations

Based on the analysis of environmental factors and dust concentration, the following general awareness suggestions can help reduce household exposure to particulate matter.

Disclaimer: These are general environmental recommendations, not medical diagnosis or treatment claims.

High Traffic Areas

  • Keep windows facing main roads closed during peak traffic hours to prevent exhaust and road dust entry.
  • Plant dense vegetation or hedges between the household and the road to act as a natural dust barrier.
  • Use air purifiers with HEPA filters indoors to reduce PM2.5 accumulation.

Nearby Construction

  • Ensure construction sites use water spraying techniques to suppress coarse dust (PM10).
  • Seal gaps in doors and windows to prevent coarse particulate matter from blowing indoors.
  • Clean indoor surfaces with damp cloths rather than dry dusting to avoid resuspending particles.

High Dust Conditions

  • Limit outdoor physical activities when local air quality index indicates high PM2.5 or PM10 levels.
  • Wear appropriate masks (like N95) if prolonged outdoor exposure is necessary during high pollution days.
  • Increase indoor humidity slightly to help settle suspended dust particles.

Household Maintenance

  • Maintain clean surroundings by regularly sweeping outdoor patios and walkways with water.
  • Monitor local air quality daily using reliable apps or websites to plan ventilation.
  • Regularly clean or replace filters in air conditioning and ventilation systems.

Health & Care Advice

Good Air Quality

Status: PM2.5 ≤ 12 µg/m³

  • Ideal conditions for outdoor activities and exercise.
  • Open windows to naturally ventilate your home.
  • No special health precautions are necessary.

Moderate Air Quality

Status: PM2.5 12.1 - 35.4 µg/m³

  • Unusually sensitive individuals should consider limiting prolonged outdoor exertion.
  • Keep windows partially closed during peak traffic.
  • If you experience respiratory symptoms, reduce exposure.

Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

Status: PM2.5 35.5 - 55.4 µg/m³

  • People with asthma or heart disease, elderly, and children should limit outdoor activities.
  • Close windows facing main roads.
  • Consider turning on indoor air purifiers.

Unhealthy / Hazardous

Status: PM2.5 > 55.4 µg/m³

  • Everyone should avoid prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion.
  • Wear an N95 mask if going outside is necessary.
  • Keep all windows closed and run air purifiers constantly.
  • Seek medical advice if you experience shortness of breath or chest pain.
