Key Statistics

Factors Affecting Dust Concentration

Distance from Road

Proximity to main roads directly impacts dust exposure levels.

Traffic Density

Higher traffic activity can contribute to increased road dust and particulate pollution.

Road Type

Highways and arterial roads generate more pollution than residential streets.

Wind Speed

Wind can disperse particles or transport dust from other areas.

Humidity

Moisture can weigh down dust, affecting its concentration in the air.

Construction Activity

Nearby construction is a major source of coarse particulate matter.

Vegetation

Trees and plants can act as natural barriers and filters for dust.

PM2.5

Fine particles that can deeply penetrate the respiratory system.

PM10

Coarse dust particles mostly generated by mechanical processes like traffic.
